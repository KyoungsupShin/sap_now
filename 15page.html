15 · ENTERPRISE AI GOVERNANCE

Autonomous agents need enterprise-grade governance

Governance is designed into how agents are built, run, and observed—not applied after execution.

BUILD

  • Agent definitionPurpose, instructions, and scope
  • Skills & toolsApproved capabilities and interfaces
  • PermissionsRoles, data boundaries, and access

RUN

  • Authentication & authorizationVerified identity and least-privilege access
  • Policy & guardrailsTool permission, approval, and execution controls
  • Data boundaryAppropriate context for each task

OBSERVE

  • Audit & traceWhat happened, why, and with which evidence
  • Logs & monitoringOperational visibility across agents and tools
  • Lifecycle managementReview, improve, and retire capabilities responsibly

Controlled autonomy means every action is bounded by identity, policy, evidence, and accountability.
