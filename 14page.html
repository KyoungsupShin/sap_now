14 · HUMAN + AGENT OPERATING MODEL

Autonomy scales with control

Humans set intent and guardrails. Agents take on more execution only when the risk and confidence allow it.

LEVEL 1

AI recommends

Agent gathers evidence and proposes the next best action.

Human decides
LEVEL 2

AI executes with approval

Agent prepares and executes an approved action.

Human approves
LEVEL 3

AI executes within guardrails

Agent resolves routine work inside defined policy, role, and tool boundaries.

Human handles exceptions
LEVEL 4

AI orchestrates processes

Multiple agents coordinate across teams and applications for an end-to-end outcome.

Human governs the system

Human intervention increases when risk is high, policy requires approval, confidence is low, or an exception occurs.
